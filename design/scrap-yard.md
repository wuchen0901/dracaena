# Scrap yard signal beat

Inside the prison grounds, after slipping past the sentry and before the perimeter wall, the robot crosses an abandoned salvage line that is still running. The conveyor carries dismantled robot shells marked with the same `07` batch number as the protagonist; a sign identifies the line as a memory erasure route. This reveals why escape matters without a dialogue scene.

Vines have started to split the gantry, but the belt and crusher still move. Past the press, a weak amber beacon marked `Coastal Relay` points farther right toward the prison wall and the coast beyond it. The segment keeps the existing move-and-jump controls and records a checkpoint once the player passes the line.

World coordinates are defined by `SALVAGE` in `SluiceWorld.ts`. The drawing uses Cocos Graphics in `ScrapYardArt.ts`; signs and beacon labels are positioned in `PuzzleController.ts`.
