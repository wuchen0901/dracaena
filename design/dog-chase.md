# Guard dog pursuit after the prison wall

The route leaves the prison after the inner sentry, salvage conveyor and crate puzzle, then continues into a dog-and-kitten rescue. At the broken outer wall, the kitten cowers above a barking dog. The player jumps onto its ledge; landing there rescues the kitten and makes the dog turn from barking to pursuit. The kitten follows automatically. A crate wedged at the gap's near edge gives the player a high step and blocks the dog, then the player and kitten cross the gap. The outside police search follows at the roadside tree. `DogChaseSegment.ts` owns the local layout and chase state. Origin is x=6300, approach is +40, pursuit trigger is +160, and the broken path spans +650..+740 at y=-140. The 90-unit gap is crossable with a normal walking jump; sprinting is optional.

At the dog approach checkpoint, the dog appears 260 units behind and barks at the kitten while the player reaches the wall ledge. If left alone, it turns and chases after 3.5 seconds. After rescue it runs right at 185 units/second. The player must jump onto the crate before the dog reaches them. It stops and barks just behind the crate and never jumps either the crate or gap. If it reaches the player before safety, the failure overlay offers archive loading. Falling into the gap restores the safe approach and resets both dog and kitten. Pause freezes their clocks.

The world model receives the dog segment, samples the kitten ledge, blocking crate, gap floor and bank walls, and leaves other puzzles independent. Terrain, grass and fences are interrupted at the same gap geometry. `DogGapArt.ts` draws the broken outer wall, crate barrier and pit. Both banks return to the same ground height.

Named automatic checkpoints are “墙外 · 犬吠逼近” and “越过断口 · 甩开追犬”. Active pursuit and caught states do not overwrite a safe snapshot. Completion is saved only after the protagonist lands safely beyond the far edge; this checkpoint also stores that the kitten was rescued. Loading it restores the kitten beside the robot and the dog barking behind the barrier; loading an earlier wall save resets the kitten to the ledge. The prison crate state remains preserved.

The built-in imagegen tool generated `assets/resources/dog/chase-bark.png` and the eight-pose kitten atlas `assets/resources/cat/follow.png`, both with transparent backgrounds. The kitten uses four frightened poses and four follow-running poses. Runtime foot anchors are visually estimated. `design/generate_dog_bark.py` creates an original synthesized two-bark audio loop with no external recording. The loop starts only after gesture unlock, fades with distance, and stops on earlier saves. Audio realism and mixing need listening review.

The kitten rescue is a new change on top of the earlier dog chase. Automated tests and Cocos preview have not yet been rerun for this addition; visual appearance and animation timing still need an in-engine review.
