# Reclaimed mechanical forest

The opening now uses a cooler green tint on the forest image layers, with translucent industrial towers and broken elevated tracks behind the canopy. Their slower parallax keeps them distant.

World-space service pipes, corroded joints, vines and a roofless maintenance shelter introduce former human activity along the banks. These Graphics decorations are non-colliding and leave the canal traversal geometry intact.

A lamp on the far bank warms and glows as the sluice fills. Its brightness derives from the existing fill state, so restored open-gate saves also restore the light.

This is a procedural first art pass, using the existing forest assets. The silhouettes, pipes and shelter need in-engine visual review alongside the painterly backgrounds before final art production. TypeScript checking and the existing traversal/animation suite pass; those checks do not establish visual quality or touch-device behavior.
